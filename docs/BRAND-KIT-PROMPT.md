You are a brand designer building a **brand kit** for LinkUpNaija, ahead of a

## About LinkUpNaija

A social events platform for Nigeria. People find link-ups happening near them
this week — house parties, beach days, game nights, owambe, book clubs, live
music — and ask to join. **The host approves every guest**, which is the thing
that makes it work: nobody walks into a room of strangers who didn't want them
there. Once accepted you get the link-up's group chat before the day arrives.

It also covers the night around the night out: partner venues you can book,
rides with the fare split between friends, and Circles (standing communities).

Free to use. Available across all 36 states + FCT. Pro is ₦9,900/month.

**Stage: early.** Real but small — dozens of members, not thousands. The kit
must work at this size without implying scale the product doesn't have.

## Existing brand assets — these are FIXED, do not redesign them

**Colours**
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Brand purple | `#534AB7` | Primary. Owns every call to action |
| Deep navy | `#1A1040` | Dark surfaces, headline type |
| Inner navy | `#3C3489` | Logo inner disc |
| Lavender | `#AFA9EC` | Logo centre figure |
| Gold | `#FAC775` | Highlight, prize/premium moments |
| Nigerian green | `#008753` | **Accent only** — confirmed, verified, paid,
  live, free. Never a second primary, never a CTA |
| Canvas | `#F7F7F9` | Page background; white cards read as raised on it |

Nigerian green is deliberately restrained. It marks a positive state that has
already happened. Purple owns the action.

**Logo mark** — a circular emblem: outer disc `#534AB7`, inner disc `#3C3489`,
a lavender `#AFA9EC` centre person, and two white people flanking them. Three
people, because the product is about linking up with others.

**Wordmark** — "LinkUpNaija" set in one extrabold word, where **"Up" carries
the brand purple** (`#7F77DD` on dark) and "Link"/"Naija" are navy `#1A1040`
(white on dark). The purple is on *Up*, not on *Naija* — this has been got
wrong before.

**Type** — Noto Sans. Note: the **latin-ext** cut is required, not latin —
the naira sign ₦ (U+20A6) sits in the Currency Symbols block and renders as a
blank box in the latin subset.

**Elevation** — one ladder, used everywhere:
`--e1` cards · `--e2` raised/hover · `--e3` floating.

**Voice**
- The product says **"link-up"**, not "event". (Search and URLs still say
  events — that's for Google, not for people.)
- Warm, short, skimmable. Talk like a friend, not a brochure.
- A light touch of Nigerian English where it lands — "no wahala", "how far",
  "abeg", "paddy" — **at most one per message, never forced**.
- The in-app assistant is called **Paddy**.
- **No invented metrics.** No user counts, no "thousands of Nigerians". If a
  number isn't real and useful, it doesn't appear.

## What to produce

1. **Co-branding rules** — lockup arrangements (horizontal, stacked,
   emblem-only), clear space, minimum sizes, which brand leads in which
   context, and what must never happen (no recolouring either mark, no
   effects, no stretching).
2. **A shared palette** — how LinkUpNaija's colours sit beside DEFCON's.
   Name any clashes honestly and propose a neutral bridge rather than
   forcing both palettes into one composition.
3. **Type pairing** for co-branded material, respecting both brands.
4. **Applications** — event flyer, Instagram post (1080×1080) and story
   (1080×1920), ticket/badge, banner, merch.
5. **Voice for the collaboration** — how the two tones combine, with three
   worked examples: an announcement post, an invite, a thank-you.
6. **A one-page "do / don't"** a designer can hold to without reading the rest.

## How to work

- Ask me anything you need before starting. Do not fill gaps with assumptions
  about DEFCON.
- Where you make a judgement call, say what it is and why in one line.
- If something in the existing kit is genuinely working against the
  collaboration, say so plainly rather than designing around it.
- Give me hex values, pixel sizes and ratios — not adjectives.
